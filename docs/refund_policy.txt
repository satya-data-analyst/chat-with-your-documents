Refund and Returns Policy

Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be
unused and in its original packaging. Digital products are non-refundable
once downloaded, except where the file is corrupted or the wrong item was
delivered.

How to request a refund
Submit a request through the account portal or email support with your order
number. We aim to respond within two business days. Approved refunds are
returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Exceptions
Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
Items marked "final sale" cannot be returned. Refunds above 1,000 dollars
require approval from a senior support agent.

Warranty
All physical products carry a 12-month warranty against manufacturing
defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage or normal wear.
