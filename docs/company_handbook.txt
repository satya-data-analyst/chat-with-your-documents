Northwind Analytics — Employee Handbook (extract)

1. Working hours
The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core hours are
10:00 to 16:00, when all staff are expected to be available. Outside core
hours, you may arrange your day flexibly, provided you meet your deadlines.

2. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote
arrangements require manager approval and are reviewed every six months.
When working remotely, you must be reachable on the company chat system
during core hours.

3. Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave, plus public
holidays. Leave must be requested at least two weeks in advance for absences
of three days or more. Unused leave may be carried over up to a maximum of
five days into the next calendar year.

4. Expenses
Reasonable expenses are reimbursed with a receipt. Meals during travel are
capped at 40 dollars per day. Travel must be booked through the company
portal at least seven days in advance where possible. Approval from your
manager is required for any single expense above 500 dollars.

5. Security
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Passwords must be at
least 12 characters and must not be reused across services. Any suspected
security incident must be reported to security@northwind.example within one
hour of discovery.
